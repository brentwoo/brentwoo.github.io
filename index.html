About me

I'm Brent, a harpist and keyboardist in the Boston, MA area. I have been performing for over 20 years in major concert venues in Boston, Seattle, and LA.

I specialize in playing piano for choirs and have worked with numerous singing groups including chamber ensembles, schools, and church choirs. I am also an active church musician supporting liturgical services on piano or organ. Let's talk about bringing music to your upcoming event!
